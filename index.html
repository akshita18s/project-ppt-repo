01 · The Problem

Travelers in India are one crisis away from danger

Foreign, solo, and remote-area travelers share the same gaps — and existing apps assume you're online.

  • 🆘
    No quick way to find the nearest police or hospital
  • 📵
    Safety info is inaccessible in low or no connectivity zones
  • 🗣️
    Language barriers block communication with locals and officials
  • 🚨
    Disaster alerts are unclear, scattered, and often missed
02 · Our Solution

One app. Six lifesavers.

SafeTrail is an offline-capable, multilingual safety companion that connects travelers to help instantly — SOS, live alerts, maps, and translation, all in one tap.

📶 Works without a data plan for the features that matter most

04 · Privacy & Security

Your data never leaves your device

🔐

Safety Pass

A short local code stored only on-device, never transmitted. It protects your emergency contacts from strangers who pick up your phone.

🛡️

Transparent permissions

Location, camera, and mic are requested clearly and only used while you're actively triggering SOS or the translator.
